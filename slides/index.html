What ACAPS told us

No platform exists. Honesty is the point.

  • Experts are informed professionals, not sources on the ground.
  • Experts apply. A committee approves.
  • Risk Radar is a dataset and an API. It should have a platform.
  • "We will get things wrong, like a prediction market; the point is honesty about it."

Bias and honesty

We measure bias, show it to the moderator, and state it in public.

  • Blind first round: ACAPS's rating is hidden (anchoring).
  • Weights from Brier scores (calibration).
  • Surprisingly-popular highlight (Prelec 2017).
  • Flags for the moderator only. Honesty box for everyone.
