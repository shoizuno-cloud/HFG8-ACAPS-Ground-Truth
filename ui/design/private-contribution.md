# Private contribution: a shared landscape

Goal: recognise anonymous expertise through a connected human story, preserving all measured facts and unknown outcomes. Final action: open private calibration. Primary mode: one real-time Three.js world, with semantic DOM copy and an SVG fallback. Native scroll remains reversible; no wheel interception or public identity.

## Art-direction studies

1. **Chalk garden, chosen.** White field, matte ceramic figures and planted terrain, soft directional shadows. Inter remains the reading and display face. A close view of one figure widens into a circle holding hands; camera composition leaves the text column clear. Warm, measured, collaborative. Green is a visual metaphor, not an environmental impact claim.
2. **Cut-paper commons.** Cool sage field, layered paper people and flat wooded paths, diffuse light without cast shadows. Larger editorial text sits beside a lateral relief. Friendly, but too close to the rejected card-layer treatment and weaker spatial continuity.
3. **Terracotta courtyard.** Sand field, clay figures on a warm architectural plaza, stronger sun and long shadows. Text is compact and inset into the composition. Human and tactile, but the earthy dominant palette would pull too far from the existing white-and-teal product.

## Style bible

White #ffffff; pale field #f3f7f3; deep teal #285e58; foliage #64866a; soft stone #d8dece; muted ink #596662. One world unit is roughly one adult torso width. Faceless, modestly rounded human silhouettes, varied muted clothing and skin tones; no caricature proportions. Materials are matte with small bevels. One soft key, ambient fill, one shadow map. Native scroll drives a slow camera arc and the group coming together. No automatic orbit, confetti or celebratory impact claims. Desktop composes the scene right of copy; mobile uses the upper scene with a quiet text surface below. The same landscape persists through every section, including outcomes and thanks.
